Table~\ref{tab:probabilistic} reports the three-level results. Full exclusion terminates in all 200 runs at each of $n=16,32$, while no empty-family termination is observed at $n=64,128,256$. All proposer-only runs reach the horizon. For equal initialization, full exclusion fails after one successful round in every run, as proved analytically; proposer-only runs reach the horizon. The exact grouped fallback is used 149 times, leaving no sampling-censored runs. A zero observed rate has a Wilson upper endpoint of 0.01885 at $N=200$, rather than proving zero risk. The solver-witness count 10/25 is deliberately not pooled with these data.
